## 📌 About the Project

"Sakhi Sikho, Sakhi Sikhao" is a grassroots community initiative designed for the women and young girls of Jharkhand, India. It creates an ecosystem connecting traditional indigenous art (Sohrai, Khovar, Paitkar, bamboo craft, embroidery, terracotta jewelry) with modern digital learning, community workshops, and conscious commerce.

> ⚠️ **Concept Stage Notice:**  
> This platform represents an early-stage concept and pilot study. All fees, stipends, and percentages (such as the proposed 30% platform share and ~₹10 registration) are proposals being validated through conversations with local women. No earnings or sales are guaranteed.

## 🌾 Key Sections & Features

1. **Bilingual Experience (हिन्दी / English)**: Dynamic language toggle with Devanagari-first typography (Baloo 2, Mukta, Kalam).
2. **Women-Only Ecosystem & Pan-India Market**:
   - Students, Teachers, Sellers, Delivery Partners, and Sangi Hub members are **verified women only**.
   - Conscious buyers across India (any gender) can shop authentic handmade folk crafts.
3. **Interactive Multi-Role Switcher (मेरी भूमिकाएँ)**: Allows a single woman's account to hold multiple roles (learn, teach, sell, deliver).
4. **Safety & Privacy**: Teacher-student interaction occurs strictly through in-platform live Q&A and doubt boxes; personal phone numbers are never shared.
5. **Six Ways to Participate**:
   - **शिक्षिका (Teacher)**: 5-step verification stepper, up to 2 hours of daily live/recorded teaching, fortnightly Sangi Hub Workshops, fair pay principles, and verified enrolment-based outreach incentives.
   - **विद्यार्थी (Student)**: 8-step journey, free demo class, transparent price list, course completion certificates, and a dedicated **Offline-Only Student Path** with lower fees.
   - **संगी हब कार्यशाला (Sangi Hub Workshop)**: Fortnightly 3–4 hour in-person learning, problem-solving, kit pickup, and material sales.
   - **प्रतियोगिता (Competitions)**: Objective judging by senior teachers with dual outcomes (Cash Prize + Optional separate platform product purchase).
   - **विक्रेता (Seller)**: 7-step process with payment only after customer purchase, supported by a clear money-flow diagram (70% artisan / 30% platform).
   - **खरीदार (Customer / Shop)**: Pan-India open digital marketplace with placeholder illustrated SVG cards.
   - **डिलीवरी साथी (Delivery Partner)**: Daytime, local neighborhood deliveries for women with independence and time.
6. **Open Decisions Strip**: Transparently outlines pilot aspects currently being validated with community members.
7. **Pilot Interest Form**: Interactive form with all 24 Jharkhand districts, role multi-select, and secure local storage saving.

## 💻 How to Run Locally

No build step or dependencies required:
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or serve using any static server:
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8085
   ```
3. Open `http://localhost:8085` in your browser.

## 🎨 Jharkhand Visual Identity

- **Folk Motifs**: Original Sohrai & Khovar wall painting line-work, Sal leaves, Palash flame flowers, deer, and peacock motifs drawn in pure inline SVG.
- **Earth & Festival Palette**: Mud-wall cream (`#F6ECD9`), Terracotta red-ochre (`#B5482A`), Palash flame orange (`#E4762B`), Haldi yellow (`#E3A82B`), and Sal green (`#2F5D3A`).
- **Original Logo**: Two stylized women's figures passing a Palash flower and spun thread within a circular folk-art ring.
